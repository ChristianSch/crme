# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operators and founders managing a small CRM: companies, people, tasks, and notes. They use it frequently as a working surface, not a reporting artifact, and need to move quickly through dense relationship data.

## Product Purpose

CRME is a practical CRM for tracking who matters, what company they belong to, what needs doing, and what has happened. Success means the user can find a person or company, understand the current context, and act on follow-ups without losing flow.

Detailed product verbs and CLI/UI/assistant parity rules live in [docs/usecases.md](docs/usecases.md).

## Brand Personality

Restrained, fast, direct. The interface should feel like a sharp internal tool: calm enough for daily use, polished enough to trust, and never decorative for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Avoid bloated enterprise CRM dashboards, colorful metric walls, generic SaaS hero aesthetics, over-abstracted productivity UI, and anything that makes simple relationship tracking feel ceremonial.

## Design Principles

- Tables and lists are the workspace; optimize for scanning and selection.
- Context should appear beside the current work, not replace it.
- Prefer quiet defaults with clear affordances over visual noise.
- Use familiar CRM patterns where they help speed and trust.
- Keep naming plain: companies, people, tasks, notes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Maintain keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast in muted states, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
